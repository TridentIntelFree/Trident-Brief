**BLUF**  
Russian forces conducted a large-scale overnight missile and drone attack on Ukraine (11–12 Sep), killing at least 11 civilians and injuring dozens while targeting industrial, logistics, and port facilities; Ukrainian air defenses intercepted most but not all targets. Houthi forces completed seizure of Perim (Mayyun) Island in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, tightening control over a key chokepoint. Belarus President Lukashenko ordered a full army inspection down to mobilization readiness. Activity in other theatres remained at baseline or routine levels with no verified consequential shifts in the window.

### EUROPE / RUSSIA-UKRAINE
- [OSINT - CONFIRMED] Al Jazeera, RFE/RL, RBC-Ukraine, Kyiv Independent and Euronews (12 Sep, morning UTC) reported Russian forces launched ~129 drones (including jet-powered Shahed-type), Iskander ballistic missiles, Kalibr cruise missiles and Kh-59/69 missiles overnight, striking Zaporizhstal steel plant in Zaporizhzhia, Interpipe in Dnipro, facilities in Kryvyi Rih, cargo vessels in Chornomorsk port and residential areas in Odesa; at least 11 killed and 96 injured across regions including Odesa, Zaporizhzhia and Dnipropetrovsk.[[1]](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/12/russia-strikes-cargo-vessels-steel-plants-across-ukraine)[[2]](https://www.rferl.org/a/ukraine-russia-strikes-odesa-zapporizhzhia-missile-shahed-drones/33852877.html)[[3]](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-attacks-kill-11-injure-96-across-ukraine-as-odesa-comes-under-mass-overnight-strike/)

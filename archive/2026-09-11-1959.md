## 1. STANDING THEATRE SWEEP

**EUROPE / RUSSIA-UKRAINE**  
- Russian jet-powered drone strikes hit two Kyiv gas stations on 11 September, killing two people (including later hospital deaths) and injuring at least 12, including a child; additional strikes damaged a residential building (eight injured) and other sites. Ukrainian officials and Kyiv authorities reported. This extends the pattern of targeting civilian logistics and energy infrastructure.[[4]](https://kyivindependent.com/russian-forces-kill-9-injure-145-across-ukraine-over-past-day-as-moscow-expands-gas-station-strikes-to-kyiv/)[[5]](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-war-latest-russia-targets-kyiv-gas-stations-for-second-day/)  
- Ukrainian strikes overnight and into 11 September killed at least three in Russia (two in Tula, one in Kursk) and damaged an Ozon logistics hub in Saratov plus industrial facilities in Volgograd; one report cited an FP-5 Flamingo missile strike on a Volgograd chemical plant. Regional Russian governors and Ukrainian sources confirmed.[[1]](https://www.reuters.com/world/russian-air-strikes-injure-two-cause-fire-9-storey-apartment-building-kyiv-2026-09-11/)[[5]](https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-war-latest-russia-targets-kyiv-gas-stations-for-second-day/)  
- Belarus President Lukashenko inspected troops and stated the army “needs shaking up.” Belarus state media reported.  

**MIDDLE EAST**  
- Israeli forces killed Hamas Khan Younis Brigade commander Mohammed Abdel Salam al-Yazouri and two other commanders in a Gaza airstrike on 10 September; Hamas confirmed the death. IDF and Hamas statements. This matches prior reporting with no new developments.[[2]](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/hamas-says-top-armed-commander-killed-by-israel-2026-09-11/)  
- [PRIORITY] Houthis seized Perim Island in the Bab el-Mandab Strait after government forces withdrew, completing control over surrounding areas and the vital shipping lane. AFP and other outlets reported. This directly affects Red Sea/Bab el-Mandeb chokepoint security.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal:Current_events/2026_September_11)  
- Houthis announced success in offensives expelling forces from six districts; Saudi East-West Crude Oil Pipeline closed temporarily after projectile attacks. Multiple outlets.  

**INDO-PACIFIC**  
- Taiwan tracked 11 Chinese military aircraft (10 entering ADIZ) and 10 naval/official ships around the island in the 24 hours to 6 a.m. on 11 September; Taiwan MND deployed monitoring assets. Routine gray-zone activity with no surge.[[7]](https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/en/news/6438029)  

**SOUTH AND CENTRAL ASIA / AFRICA / AMERICAS / STRATEGIC AND SPACE / ECONOMIC AND ENERGY PRESSURE**

**Assessment**  
Escalation in Ukraine-Russia deep strikes on economic targets continues without shifting front lines, increasing pressure on both sides’ logistics and civilian infrastructure while testing air defenses. Houthi consolidation in the Bab el-Mandab improves their leverage over shipping and energy routes, raising risks of further disruption or international response; this is the clearest strategic shift in the window.

## 2. TECHNOLOGY AND CYBERSECURITY

- CISA added three new vulnerabilities to the KEV catalog on 11 September due to active exploitation: CVE-2026-42016 and CVE-2026-42018 (JFrog Artifactory authorization/authentication issues) and CVE-2026-84869 (ConnectWise ScreenConnect privilege management flaw). CISA alert. These join prior edge-device exposures and require immediate federal remediation.[[3]](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/11/cisa-adds-three-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)  

**Assessment**  
Continued focus on perimeter and management appliances as high-value targets; the JFrog additions extend the pattern of supply-chain-adjacent tools under active attack, increasing urgency for patching in both government and enterprise environments.

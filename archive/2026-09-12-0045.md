## 2. TECHNOLOGY AND CYBERSECURITY
- [SIGINT - VERIFIED] CISA (11 Sep, official alert) added three vulnerabilities to the KEV catalog due to active exploitation: CVE-2026-42016 and CVE-2026-42018 (JFrog Artifactory authorization/authentication issues) and CVE-2026-84869 (ConnectWise ScreenConnect privilege management/authorization flaw allowing unauthorized file transfer and execution). Federal agencies directed to remediate per BOD 26-04 timelines.

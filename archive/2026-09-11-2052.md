## 1. STANDING THEATRE SWEEP

### EUROPE / RUSSIA-UKRAINE
- [OSINT - CONFIRMED] Multiple outlets including The Guardian, Al Jazeera, Kyiv Post, and RBC-Ukraine (11 Sep, morning–afternoon UTC) reported Russian jet-powered Shahed-type drones striking two Ukrnafta gas stations in Kyiv’s Dniprovskyi and Obolonskyi districts around 09:10–10:07 local time. Two people killed (one at scene, one in hospital); injuries rose to at least nine, including a rescuer hit in a repeat strike. Mayor Vitali Klitschko and Naftogaz confirmed targets; Zelensky called the attacks “increasingly terrorist in nature.” This follows a 10 September strike and expands pressure on civilian logistics in the capital.[[1]](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/sep/11/russia-kyiv-petrol-stations-jet-powered-drones-ukraine)[[2]](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/11/russian-strikes-kill-two-in-kyiv-as-moscow-targets-capitals-fuel-stations)[[3]](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/84304)
- [OSINT - CONFIRMED] The Moscow Times, Ukrinform, and Kyiv Independent (11 Sep, overnight/early morning UTC) reported Ukrainian drones striking an Ozon logistics warehouse and Rosneft oil refinery in Saratov. Ozon confirmed the attack, suspended operations, and saw shares drop 5%; fires were visible via NASA FIRMS data. Saratov Governor noted “civilian infrastructure” damage with no casualties. The refinery has been repeatedly targeted for its military fuel supply role.[[4]](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/09/11/ukrainian-drone-barrage-sets-ozon-warehouse-in-saratov-ablaze-a93684)[[5]](https://www.ukrinform.net/rubric-ato/4163171-ukrainian-forces-strike-saratov-oil-refinery-in-russia.html)
- [OSINT - CONFIRMED] Ukrainska Pravda, LIGA.net, and Kyiv Independent (11 Sep, afternoon UTC) reported Belarus President Alexander Lukashenko inspecting troops at the Obuz-Lesnovsky training ground and stating the army “needs shaking up,” with readiness checks extending to the entire armed forces “up to and including mobilization.” He framed the drills as wartime-style preparation “so that there is no war” and noted prior inspections of tank, motor rifle, and artillery units. Belarus state media (BelTA) carried the remarks.[[6]](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/09/11/8052980/)[[7]](https://news.liga.net/en/world/news/the-army-needs-to-be-shaken-up-all-the-way-to-mobilization-troops-in-belarus-will-be-inspected)

### MIDDLE EAST
- [OSINT - CONFIRMED] [PRIORITY] Reuters, BBC, Euronews, and AFP-sourced reports (11 Sep, morning–afternoon UTC) stated Houthi forces reached and seized Perim (Mayyun) Island in the Bab el-Mandab Strait after Yemeni government forces withdrew the previous day. The group also took the facing mainland town of Dhubab, completing control over the strait area and much of Yemen’s Red Sea coast following earlier captures of Mokha and Hanish islands. Houthi statements claimed success in expelling forces from six districts and asserted safe navigation except for Saudi vessels. Government sources indicated intent to retake territory.[[8]](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c23x72yx2rvo)[[9]](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/yemens-houthis-reach-strategic-island-mouth-vital-shipping-lane-2026-09-11/)[[10]](https://www.euronews.com/2026/09/11/houthis-seize-yemeni-island-in-bab-el-mandeb-taking-control-of-the-strait)

## 2. TECHNOLOGY AND CYBERSECURITY
- [SIGINT - VERIFIED] CISA (11 Sep, official alert) added three vulnerabilities to the KEV catalog due to active exploitation: CVE-2026-42016 and CVE-2026-42018 (JFrog Artifactory authorization/authentication issues) and CVE-2026-84869 (ConnectWise ScreenConnect improper privilege management). Federal agencies must prioritize remediation per BOD 26-04. These extend the pattern of edge/perimeter and supply-chain-adjacent tools under attack.[[12]](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/11/cisa-adds-three-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

## 3. HOMELAND AND INFRASTRUCTURE
Quiet in window. No incidents affecting civil aviation, rail, ports, power, water, or telecoms reported. No change to domestic critical infrastructure posture.

**Assessment:** The Kyiv gas-station campaign and Saratov strikes represent continued mutual deep-strike escalation focused on economic/logistics targets rather than immediate front-line shifts, raising civilian risk and supply pressure on both sides without altering territorial control. Houthi consolidation of the Bab el-Mandab represents the most consequential shift, pairing with Hormuz constraints to increase global shipping and energy leverage for the Iran-aligned axis and likely prompting expanded coalition monitoring or response. Belarus inspections signal heightened readiness posture amid regional tensions but remain declaratory for now. CISA’s KEV additions reinforce the priority on patching exposed management appliances.
